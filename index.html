Binomial Distribution

With 100 independent packets and Normal-network probability p = 0.80, total deliveries follow a Binomial distribution.

MODEL NOTE

Assumptions of the model

  1. The number of packets is fixed.
  2. Every packet has two outcomes: success or failure.
  3. Every packet has the same success probability.
  4. Packet outcomes are independent.

This is a probability-based educational simulation. It does not transmit real network packets or measure real Internet reliability. Real networks can have correlated losses caused by congestion, interference, and routing.
